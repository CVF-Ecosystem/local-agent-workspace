# Document frames (starting points)

The frames below use blanks `[...]`. They are not an approved template of any organisation; use the
organisation's own if there is one. The document text itself is in Vietnamese, so the literal Vietnamese
wording is kept and the instructions are in English. The two-column head (issuing body on the left,
national title on the right) is made with a borderless table when building a Word file.

## Common head

```text
[PARENT BODY, if any]                         CỘNG HÒA XÃ HỘI CHỦ NGHĨA VIỆT NAM
[ISSUING UNIT]                                   Độc lập - Tự do - Hạnh phúc
Số: [number]/[symbol]                      [Place], ngày [dd] tháng [mm] năm [yyyy]
```

## Official letter (công văn)

```text
V/v [short summary]

Kính gửi: [Receiving body/unit]

[Basis or reason for sending the letter.]
[Content of the request/notice, one idea per paragraph.]
[The specific request and deadline, only when confirmed.]
Trân trọng./.

Nơi nhận:                                   [SIGNER'S TITLE]
- Như trên;                                 [Signature, full name: filled in by the signer]
- [Other recipients];
- Lưu: VT, [department].
```

## Submission (tờ trình)

```text
TỜ TRÌNH
[Về việc ...]

Kính trình: [Authority with competence]

1. Necessity / basis: [legal, practical, sources]
2. Proposed content: [stated clearly, with sourced figures]
3. Assessment of impact / options (if any): [state only what has a basis]
4. Recommendation: [what is requested for approval]

[List of attached documents, if any]
```

## Report (báo cáo)

```text
BÁO CÁO
[Về việc / reporting period]

Kính gửi: [Recipient]

I. Kết quả thực hiện: [what was achieved in the period, sourced figures]
II. Tồn tại, vướng mắc: [only what is real; "không có" if there is none]
III. Kế hoạch / kiến nghị: [next work; deadline and owner only when confirmed]
```

## Notice (thông báo)

```text
THÔNG BÁO
[Về việc ...]

[Content of the notice: what changes, from when, who is affected, what must be done, whom to contact.]
```

## Meeting minutes

```text
MEETING MINUTES
[Subject ...]

Time: [hour, date]. Place: [venue].
Attendees: [list with titles].
Chair: [name, title]. Secretary: [name].

Content: [in the order of the agenda, objective narration].
Conclusions, resolutions: [what was agreed; tasks, people and deadlines only when settled].

Chair                                     Secretary
[Signature, name]                         [Signature, name]
```

Meeting minutes usually have no "To:" or "Basis"; the signature block has the chair and the secretary side by side. Official minutes may add a document number and "Recipients" under the rule that applies; minutes recording an internal event may drop those two parts, but tell the user it was a dropped item. Drafting minutes from meeting notes: `meeting-minutes`.

## Plan

Follows the template for documents with a type name (Template 1.4, Annex I, Decree 30/2020/ND-CP); the layout below is a
common way to divide it, not a rule. The document text stays in Vietnamese; the English here explains each part.

```text
KẾ HOẠCH (PLAN)
[Summary of the plan]

[Basis, if any; one per line.]

I. MỤC ĐÍCH, YÊU CẦU (PURPOSE, REQUIREMENTS)
1. Purpose: [what the plan aims to achieve]
2. Requirements: [conditions and standards to meet]

II. NỘI DUNG (CONTENT)
1. [Task]: [work, expected result]

III. TỔ CHỨC THỰC HIỆN (IMPLEMENTATION)
1. Assignment: [unit, person in charge; only when confirmed]
2. Budget (if any): [source, amount; with a source]
3. Schedule: [milestones; only when confirmed]
```

## Decision (individual, direct provisions)

Follows Template 1.2, Annex I, Decree 30/2020/ND-CP. A decision issuing a regulation or rules is Template 1.3 (its articles
only state the issuance, effect and responsibility for implementation; the content is in the attached document).

```text
QUYẾT ĐỊNH (DECISION)
Về việc [summary]

[ISSUING AUTHORITY, for example GIÁM ĐỐC CÔNG TY ...]

Căn cứ [document establishing the authority];
Căn cứ [document serving as the basis];
Theo đề nghị của [unit/person proposing].

QUYẾT ĐỊNH:

Điều 1. [Content of the decision.]
Điều 2. [Effect, for example "Quyết định này có hiệu lực kể từ ngày ký."]
Điều 3. [Units and persons responsible for implementation.]/.

Nơi nhận:                                   [AUTHORITY, TITLE OF THE SIGNER]
- Như Điều 3;                               [Signature, name: filled in by the signer]
- Lưu: VT, [department].
```

When the head of the body holds the authority, write the head's title; when it belongs to a collective leadership or the body
itself, write that collective or the body's name. Basis lines are italic, each ending with a semicolon, the last with a full stop.

## Invitation

Follows Template 1.7, Annex I, Decree 30/2020/ND-CP; symbol `Số: [number]/GM-[unit abbreviation]`.

```text
GIẤY MỜI (INVITATION)
[Summary of the meeting]

[Inviting unit] trân trọng kính mời: [body, or name, title and unit of the invitee]
Tới dự: [name and subject of the meeting or workshop]
Chủ trì: [chair]
Thời gian: [time, date]
Địa điểm: [venue]
[Points to note: documents to prepare, confirmation of attendance.]/.

Nơi nhận:                                   [AUTHORITY, TITLE OF THE SIGNER]
- [Recipient];                              [Signature, name: filled in by the signer]
- Lưu: VT, [department].
```

## Order of "Basis" lines

List the basis in descending order of authority (charter or regulation, resolution, a superior's direction, specific technical records), each as a paragraph starting "Pursuant to ..."; the last sentence leads into the content (for example "... respectfully reports as follows:"). A common closing is "Trân trọng./." ("Respectfully."); short letters and submissions may skip a long basis block. A basis must come from the user or have been looked up; do not add a basis document on your own.

## Notes when using the frames

Do not fill in the document number, issue date, the signer's name, title or signing authority for the
user. If the unit has its own decision or plan template, use it. Letters of introduction, urgent dispatches and leave
permits have templates in Annex I of Decree 30/2020/ND-CP (Templates 1.6, 1.8, 1.10); a power of attorney has no template
in this decree.
