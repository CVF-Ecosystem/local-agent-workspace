# Layout parameters from Annex I of Decree 30/2020/ND-CP

Checked on 2026-10-12 against a scan of Decree 30/2020/ND-CP (Annex I, Part I, sections I, II and V). Use when building or
checking a Word file for a document in the state format. The unit's own records-management regulation (if any) still comes
before this table. Before issuing, check that the decree is still in force, or say it was not checked.

## General rules

| Item | Parameter |
|---|---|
| Paper | A4 (210 x 297 mm), portrait; a document with tables that are not made into separate annexes may be landscape. |
| Margins | Top and bottom 20 - 25 mm; left 30 - 35 mm; right 15 - 20 mm. |
| Font | Times New Roman, Unicode per TCVN 6909:2001, black. |
| Page numbers | Arabic numerals, size 13 - 14, upright, centred in the top margin; not shown on the first page. |
| Body | Lower case, justified, upright, size 13 - 14; first line indented 1 cm or 1.27 cm; at least 6 pt between paragraphs; line spacing at least single, at most 1.5 lines. |
| Consistent sizes | Sizes of the components rise or fall together within one document (for example national title 13, motto 14, place and date 14; or 12, 13, 13). |

## Format components (section V, "Type samples and layout details")

| Component | Case | Size | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| National title | Capitals | 12 - 13 | Upright, bold |
| Motto | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold; spaced hyphens between phrases; a horizontal rule below as long as the line |
| Direct parent body | Capitals | 12 - 13 | Upright |
| Issuing body | Capitals | 12 - 13 | Upright, bold; a rule below 1/3 - 1/2 of the line, centred |
| Number and symbol | Lower case | 13 | Upright |
| Place and date | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Italic |
| Document type name | Capitals | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold |
| Summary (typed documents) | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold; a rule below 1/3 - 1/2 of the line |
| Summary of an official letter ("V/v ...") | Lower case | 12 - 13 | Upright; centred under the number and symbol, 6 pt apart |
| Basis for issuance | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Italic; one basis per line, each ending with a semicolon, the last with a full stop |
| "Phần", "Chương" and number (Roman numerals) | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold, centred; title in capitals, bold, just below |
| "Mục", "Tiểu mục" and number (Arabic) | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold, centred; title in capitals, bold, just below |
| Article | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold; first line indented 1 cm or 1.27 cm; Arabic number followed by a full stop |
| Clause | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright (a clause with a title has a bold title) |
| Point | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright; a), b), c) in Vietnamese alphabetical order |
| Signing authority (TM., KT., TL., TUQ., Q.) | Capitals | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold |
| Signer's title | Capitals | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold |
| Signer's name | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold |
| "Kính gửi" (To) and addressees | Lower case | 13 - 14 | Upright; several addressees each on their own line starting with a dash |
| The word "Nơi nhận:" (Recipients) | Lower case | 12 | Italic, bold |
| Recipient list | Lower case | 11 | Upright |
| "Phụ lục" (Annex) and number | Lower case | 14 | Upright, bold |
| Annex title | Capitals | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold |
| Urgency mark (HỎA TỐC, THƯỢNG KHẨN, KHẨN) | Capitals | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold, boxed |
| Drafter symbol, number of copies | Lower case | 11 | Upright |
| Address, phone, e-mail | Lower case | 11 - 12 | Upright |
| Circulation note | Capitals | 13 - 14 | Upright, bold, boxed |

The national title and motto are one single line apart; so are the parent body and the issuing body.

## Templates in Annex I

Template 1.1 Resolution (individual); 1.2 Decision (individual) with direct provisions; 1.3 Decision with indirect provisions;
1.4 Document with a type name (used for reports, submissions, plans, notices...); 1.5 Official letter; 1.6 Urgent dispatch;
1.7 Invitation; 1.8 Letter of introduction; 1.9 Minutes; 1.10 Leave permit. A power of attorney has no template in this
decree; it is a civil transaction, so use the unit's template or look up the specific rules.

## When building a Word file

- With a 30 mm left margin and a 15 - 20 mm right margin, the text width is 16 - 16.5 cm. Borderless two-column table for the
  heading block: about 6 cm (left) and 10 cm (right). If the national title wraps, use size 12 (still within the allowed
  range) before narrowing the character spacing.
- The default DOCX layout for internal documents (`office-documents`, `references/docx-default-layout.md`) uses values that
  fall inside the ranges above, so it can be shared; if another template sets paragraph spacing below 6 pt or a first-line
  indent other than 1 cm or 1.27 cm, do not use it for documents in this format.
