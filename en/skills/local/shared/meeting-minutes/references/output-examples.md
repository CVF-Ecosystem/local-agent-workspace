# Output example: meeting minutes

An illustration; names and content are fictional. Not a mandatory template; use the organisation's own if there is one.

## Input (raw notes)

> Weekly meeting, department A, Monday. Present: Nam, Lan, Hung. Nam reported 12 files outstanding, Lan
> said she'll probably finish this week but isn't sure. Agreed to move 5 files to Hung. New form: nobody
> knows who does it, look at it again.

## Output

**Weekly check-in, department A** (time and place: not stated)
Attendees: Nam, Lan, Hung

**Decisions**

- Move 5 outstanding files to Hung for handling.

**Action items**

| Task | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Receive and handle the 5 files moved over | Hung | not stated |
| Finish the remaining outstanding files (Lan expects this week, not certain) | Lan | not stated |

**Discussion**

- Nam reported 12 outstanding files at present.

**Open points**

- Who owns the new form: not yet determined, to be looked at again.

## What to notice

"Probably finish this week" does not become a committed deadline. A task with no clear owner goes under
"Open points" and is not assigned to anyone. A cell that was not mentioned reads "not stated".
