# Source access record template

Design template, not access authority or proof of verification. Mark unknowns; do not record passwords, keys or sensitive payloads.

- Origin/agency and page URL:
- Endpoint, method, payload sent and data received:
- Access mechanism, permission/terms and scope of use, automation and distribution:
- Capability and technical limits, rate, bytes, redirects and hosts:
- TLS and permitted authentication mechanism:
- Evidence, file/final URL, revision/hash, check date and observed outcome or refusal reason:
- Unchecked items or confirmation needed:

Official origin, permission and verified content/validity are separate. Assess POST by actual effect, endpoint, payload and specific permission; do not blanket allow or ban POST. Do not bypass access controls. This template creates no endpoint or adapter.
