# Signs of "machine-like" style common in Vietnamese

A reference list for noticing where text sounds formulaic. It is not a test of "was this written by AI",
and a single sign is not an error. Edit only when a sentence is really less clear or padded. The examples
are Vietnamese because the skill edits Vietnamese text; English glosses follow in brackets.

## Content

| Sign | Example | How to fix |
|---|---|---|
| Generic inflation of importance | "đóng vai trò then chốt", "ngày càng quan trọng trong bối cảnh hiện nay" (plays a key role; ever more important today) | State the concrete effect or drop it. |
| Vague sources | "nhiều chuyên gia cho rằng", "theo các nghiên cứu" (many experts believe; studies show) | Name a specific source; if none, drop the claim or say plainly there is no source. |
| Promotional language | "toàn diện", "vượt trội", "tối ưu", "đột phá" (comprehensive, outstanding, optimal, breakthrough) | Replace with a fact or a figure that can be checked. |
| Empty analysis at the end of a sentence | "..., góp phần nâng cao hiệu quả và chất lượng" (contributing to higher efficiency and quality) | Drop the filler clause, or state a measurable effect. |
| A formulaic "challenges and outlook" section | "Dù còn nhiều thách thức, tương lai đầy hứa hẹn" (despite many challenges, the future is promising) | Write the real difficulties and next steps if there are any. |

## Wording and structure

- **Forced triplets:** "nhanh chóng, hiệu quả và chính xác" (fast, effective and accurate). Keep the ideas that mean something on their own.
- **"Not only ... but also ..." and "not ... but rather ..." repeated** ("không chỉ... mà còn...", "không phải... mà là...") within one paragraph.
- **The same sentence opener** ("Bên cạnh đó", "Ngoài ra", "Đặc biệt": besides, in addition, especially) in several sentences running.
- **Needless connectors:** "có thể nói rằng", "cần lưu ý rằng", "điều quan trọng là" (it can be said that; note that; what matters is).
- **Subjectless passive:** "được thực hiện", "được triển khai" (is carried out; is rolled out) when you need to know who did it. In
  administrative text, the legitimate passive (such as "được phê duyệt", approved) is used normally.
- **Repeating the heading in the first sentence**, or announcing what is coming ("Sau đây là ba điểm chính": here are three main points).
- **Cliched endings:** "Tóm lại, ... mở ra nhiều cơ hội" (in short, ... opens many opportunities), "Hy vọng thông tin hữu ích" (hope this is useful).

## Layout

- Too much bold, or lists in which every item opens with a bold phrase and a colon.
- Emoji in business text.
- Title Case headings while the text uses ordinary lower case.
- Overused long dashes; fix only when the user's own sample does not use them.

## Leftover chatbot replies

"Dưới đây là...", "Tất nhiên rồi!", "Tôi không có khả năng truy cập thông tin mới nhất" (here is...; of course!; I cannot access the latest
information), praise of the question, an invitation to ask more at the end. Delete or replace with the content that is needed.

## Not counted as errors

- Standard legal and administrative wording: "căn cứ", "theo đó", "chịu trách nhiệm", "kính gửi" (pursuant to; accordingly; responsible for; respectfully to).
- Technical terms, codes, form names, verbatim quotations.
- A long sentence that is still clear; lists and tables when the content really is a list or a table.
- The writer's voice already present in the sample the user gave: keep it.

## How to use

Read it once, fix what makes a sentence hard to understand or padded, keep the idea, the figures and the
degree of certainty, then stop. Do not add facts, opinions or details to make a sentence "lively".
