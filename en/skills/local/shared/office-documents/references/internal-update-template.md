# Reference template - Internal update

For a short announcement, a progress report or a periodic update. You may write it as a few paragraphs
instead of keeping all the headings. Prefer the organisation's template and voice.

## [Topic] - [Period / date]

**Main situation:** [What the reader needs to know first; give facts when there are any.]

**Completed:** [Results, not just a list of activities.]

**Next:** [Actions; owner and timing if confirmed.]

**Support / decisions needed:** [State the problem and what the reader must do; drop it if none.]

**Related sources or documents:** [Paths / file names that are needed.]

Do not infer causes from a single figure. State what is unconfirmed instead of turning a guess into a
conclusion. Do not add new commitments or deadlines on your own.
